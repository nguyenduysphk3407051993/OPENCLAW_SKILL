# Cinematic Techniques Reference

Tài liệu tham khảo chi tiết về kỹ thuật điện ảnh để viết prompt video chuyên nghiệp.

## 1. Camera Movement (Chuyển động máy quay)

### Movement cơ bản

| Movement | Mô tả | Cảm xúc tạo ra | Khi nào dùng |
|----------|-------|----------------|--------------|
| **Static / Locked-off** | Máy quay đứng yên hoàn toàn | Trang trọng, quan sát, tense | Đối thoại, suy ngẫm |
| **Pan left/right** | Xoay ngang trên trục dọc | Giới thiệu không gian | Establishing shot |
| **Tilt up/down** | Xoay dọc trên trục ngang | Khám phá, reveal | Reveal nhân vật/landscape |
| **Dolly in / Push in** | Tiến sát chủ thể | Tăng tension, intimate | Khoảnh khắc emotional |
| **Dolly out / Pull back** | Lùi xa chủ thể | Cô đơn, isolation, reveal context | Kết phim, reveal scale |
| **Tracking / Trucking** | Di chuyển song song với subject | Năng động, theo dõi | Action, walking-and-talking |
| **Crane / Jib** | Lên xuống theo phương dọc | Hoành tráng, godly view | Mở phim, big reveal |
| **Drone / Aerial** | Quay từ trên cao | Epic, scale, location showcase | Travel, opening shot |
| **Handheld** | Cầm tay, rung nhẹ | Realistic, urgent, documentary | Action, horror, war |
| **Steadicam** | Mượt như dolly nhưng theo người | Theo nhân vật trong không gian phức tạp | One-take scenes |
| **Gimbal** | Mượt 3-axis, hiện đại | Modern, smooth | Vlog, action |
| **Whip pan** | Pan cực nhanh | Transition, năng lượng | TikTok cuts, action |
| **Zoom in/out** | Thay đổi tiêu cự | Tâm lý, focus shift | Drama, comedy |
| **Dolly zoom (Vertigo)** | Dolly + zoom ngược chiều | Distortion, anxiety, dread | Horror, psychological |
| **Orbit / 360°** | Xoay quanh chủ thể | Showcase, dramatic | Product, hero shot |
| **Rack focus** | Đổi điểm focus | Chuyển attention | Storytelling shift |

### Phrasing cho prompt
- "Slow dolly in toward the subject's face"
- "Tracking shot following the runner from the side"
- "Crane shot ascending from ground level to bird's eye view"
- "Handheld documentary-style camera with subtle shake"
- "Smooth gimbal tracking through the marketplace"
- "Whip pan transition to reveal..."
- "Drone shot pulling back to reveal vast landscape"

## 2. Shot Types & Composition

### Shot sizes (theo khoảng cách)

| Shot | Khung hình | Ứng dụng |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Extreme Wide Shot (EWS)** | Subject rất nhỏ trong không gian rộng | Establishing, isolation |
| **Wide Shot (WS)** | Toàn thân + môi trường | Action, location |
| **Medium Wide Shot (MWS)** | Từ đầu gối lên | Group scene, movement |
| **Medium Shot (MS)** | Từ thắt lưng lên | Đối thoại tiêu chuẩn |
| **Medium Close-Up (MCU)** | Từ ngực lên | News, interview |
| **Close-Up (CU)** | Mặt + vai | Cảm xúc nhân vật |
| **Extreme Close-Up (ECU)** | Mắt, miệng, chi tiết nhỏ | Tense, reveal detail |
| **Insert / Detail shot** | Chi tiết vật/hành động | Cutaway, transition |

### Composition rules

- **Rule of thirds**: Subject ở 1/3 khung hình
- **Center composition**: Wes Anderson style, symmetrical
- **Leading lines**: Đường dẫn mắt người xem
- **Frame within frame**: Cửa sổ, cửa, gương
- **Negative space**: Khoảng trống tạo isolation
- **Headroom**: Khoảng trống trên đầu nhân vật
- **Lead room / Nose room**: Không gian phía trước hướng nhìn

### Phrasing cho prompt
- "Medium close-up framed with rule of thirds"
- "Symmetrical center composition Wes Anderson style"
- "Wide establishing shot with leading lines drawing toward subject"
- "Over-the-shoulder shot revealing what character sees"

| Angle | Hiệu ứng |
|-------|----------|
| **Eye-level** | Trung tính, đồng cảm |
| **Low angle** | Power, dominance, hero |
| **High angle** | Yếu đuối, vulnerability, observed |

| **Worm's eye** | Extreme low, towering |

## 4. Lighting Setup

### Three-point lighting (chuẩn)
- **Key light**: Ánh sáng chính, mạnh nhất
- **Fill light**: Lấp shadow, mềm hơn key
- **Back light / Rim light**: Tách subject khỏi nền

### Lighting styles

| Style | Đặc điểm | Phù hợp |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **High-key** | Sáng, ít shadow | Comedy, commercial, beauty |
| **Low-key** | Tối, contrast cao | Noir, horror, drama |
| **Natural light** | Sun, window | Documentary, realism |
| **Golden hour** | Ấm, mềm | Romance, nostalgia |
| **Blue hour** | Xanh, mờ | Dreamy, melancholic |
| **Practical lighting** | Đèn trong cảnh (lamp, neon) | Cyberpunk, intimate |
| **Hard light** | Shadow rõ, sharp | Drama, fashion |
| **Soft light / Diffused** | Shadow mềm | Beauty, portrait |
| **Backlight / Silhouette** | Subject tối, nền sáng | Mystery, dramatic |
| **Rembrandt** | Triangle light on cheek | Portrait, classical |
| **Split lighting** | Nửa sáng nửa tối | Conflict, duality |
| **Neon / Cyberpunk** | Magenta + cyan, glow | Futuristic, urban |
| **Fire / Candlelight** | Ấm, flickering | Intimate, period |

### Phrasing
- "Soft golden hour lighting from camera left"
- "Low-key dramatic lighting with hard rim light"
- "Cinematic three-point lighting with rim separation"
- "Practical neon signage providing magenta and cyan accents"

| Grade | Đặc điểm | Phim tham khảo |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Teal & Orange** | Skin ấm + shadow lạnh | Hollywood blockbuster |
| **Bleach bypass** | Desaturated, contrast cao | Saving Private Ryan |
| **Warm tones** | Vàng, cam dominant | Amélie, La La Land |
| **Cool tones** | Xanh dominant | The Social Network, Joker |
| **Monochrome** | Đen trắng | Schindler's List, The Lighthouse |
| **Sepia** | Nâu vàng vintage | Western, period |
| **Vibrant pop** | Saturated mạnh | Wes Anderson, Edgar Wright |
| **Muted pastels** | Wes Anderson palette | Grand Budapest Hotel |

- **Greta Gerwig**: Warm naturalistic
- **Roger Deakins**: Naturalistic, soft

## 6. Lens & Depth of Field

| Lens | Effect | Use case |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Ultra-wide 14-24mm** | Distortion, immersive | Architecture, action |
| **Wide 35mm** | Cinematic standard | Most narrative |
| **Standard 50mm** | Natural perspective | Drama, dialogue |
| **Portrait 85mm** | Compressed, beautiful bokeh | Beauty, close-up |
| **Telephoto 135mm+** | Compressed background | Sports, surveillance |
| **Macro** | Extreme detail | Insert shots |
| **Anamorphic** | Wide cinematic + horizontal flare + oval bokeh | Hollywood blockbuster |
| **Fisheye** | Extreme distortion | Action, music video |

### Depth of field
- **Shallow DOF**: Blurred background, isolated subject (f/1.4 - f/2.8)
- **Deep focus**: Everything sharp (f/8+)
- **Rack focus**: Shift focus during shot

### Bokeh
- Smooth bokeh = nice rounded out-of-focus highlights
- Anamorphic bokeh = oval-shaped
- Swirly bokeh = vintage Petzval-style

### Phrasing
- "Anamorphic 50mm lens with horizontal flares and oval bokeh"
- "35mm wide cinematic lens with shallow depth of field"
- "85mm portrait lens with creamy bokeh background"

## 7. Motion & Speed

| Speed | Phrasing | Use case |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Real-time** | Standard 24fps | Default narrative |
| **Slow motion** | 60fps, 120fps, 240fps | Emotional moments, action impact |
| **Hyper slow** | 1000fps+ | Bullet time, water splash |
| **Time-lapse** | Sky, city, nature evolution | Establishing, transitions |
| **Hyperlapse** | Moving time-lapse | Travel, opening |
| **Speed ramp** | Smooth transition normal→slow | Action, music videos |
| **Frozen / Bullet time** | 360° frozen moment | Matrix, action |
| **Reverse motion** | Played backward | Surreal, dramatic |

## 9. Audio (Cho Veo 3 và các tool có sound)

### Components

1. **Ambient sound**: Wind, rain, traffic, ocean, forest
2. **Foley**: Footsteps, fabric, door creaks
3. **SFX**: Explosions, swooshes, impacts
4. **Music**: Genre, instruments, mood
5. **Dialogue**: Speech, whispers, breathing

### Phrasing
- "Ambient rain pattering on window with distant thunder"
- "Foley footsteps on gravel, fabric rustling"
- "Sweeping orchestral score building to crescendo"
- "Lo-fi hip-hop beat with vinyl crackle"
- "Dialogue: 'I never thought it would end like this' whispered softly"

## 10. Pacing & Editing Cues

| Pacing | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Slow contemplative** | Long takes, minimal cuts |
| **Medium narrative** | Standard storytelling |
| **Fast-paced** | Quick cuts, energy |
| **Music video pacing** | Rhythmic with beat |
| **TikTok pacing** | Hook in 1s, cuts every 2-3s |
| **One-take continuous** | No cuts, single shot |

## Glossary chuyên ngành

- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 (widescreen), 2.39:1 (cinemascope), 9:16 (vertical), 1:1 (square)
- **Frame rate**: 24fps (cinematic), 30fps (TV), 60fps (smooth/sports), 120fps+ (slow-mo)
- **Resolution**: HD (1080p), 4K (3840×2160), 6K, 8K
- **Codec/Format**: ProRes (pro), H.264 (web), H.265 (efficient)
- **Letterbox**: Black bars top/bottom for cinematic ratio
- **Pillarbox**: Black bars left/right when 16:9 on 21:9
- **Crash zoom**: Extremely fast zoom (Edgar Wright signature)
- **Match cut**: Cut between visually similar frames
- **J-cut / L-cut**: Audio leading/trailing video transition
