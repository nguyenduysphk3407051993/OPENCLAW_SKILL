## 1. TikTok / Reels / Shorts

### Specs
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 vertical
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 minimum, 4K preferred
- **Duration**: 7-15s sweet spot, max 60s-3m
- **FPS**: 30 standard, 60 cho slow-motion smooth
- **Safe zone**: Tránh top 10% (header) và bottom 20% (caption + UI)

### Đặc điểm thành công
1. **Hook trong 1 giây đầu** — visual ấn tượng/câu hỏi
2. **Pattern interrupt** — đổi cảnh/zoom mỗi 2-3s
3. **Vertical-first composition** — chủ thể giữa khung
4. **Trend audio** — match với âm nhạc trending
5. **Loop-friendly** — kết nối lại với đầu video

### Template TikTok Ad/Content
```
Vertical 9:16 [hook moment description in first second], 
medium close-up of [subject] [doing eye-catching action],
fast-paced TikTok aesthetic with quick visual changes,
vibrant trendy color grading [colors like peach, lavender, neon mint],
soft studio lighting with dynamic angle changes,
energetic and engaging mood,
60fps smooth motion with strategic slow-motion moments,
optimized for mobile vertical scrolling with subject centered,
upbeat trending music beat, ASMR sound design,
4K vertical, 1080x1920, no text overlay (added in post)
```

### Phong cách TikTok phổ biến

| Style | Mô tả | Ví dụ |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **POV** | First-person view | "POV: bạn vào quán mới mở" |
| **Day in the life** | Vlog style cô đọng | Routine montage |
| **Transformation** | Trước-sau | Glow up, makeup |
| **Tutorial** | How-to nhanh | Recipe, tips |
| **Storytelling** | Kể chuyện ngắn | "Tôi đã làm gì hôm nay" |
| **Trend dance** | Theo nhạc trending | Choreography |
| **Behind the scenes** | Hậu trường | Process, work |
| **Aesthetic** | Lifestyle đẹp | Coffee, study, morning |

## 2. YouTube Long-form

### Specs
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 horizontal
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (HD), 3840x2160 (4K)
- **Duration**: 8-15 phút sweet spot, max không giới hạn
- **FPS**: 24 (cinematic), 30 (vlog), 60 (gaming/sports)

### Phong cách

| Genre | Visual Style |
|-------|--------------|
| **Vlog** | Handheld, natural light, b-roll | 
| **Cinematic vlog** | Sony FX3 look, color grade, music | 
| **Educational** | Clean, talking head + b-roll, motion graphics |
| **Tutorial** | Screen + face-cam, clear lighting |
| **Gaming** | Webcam + gameplay, fast cuts |
| **Documentary** | Handheld, interview, archival |
| **Review** | Product close-ups, demos |

### Template YouTube Cinematic Vlog
```
Cinematic medium shot of [subject doing relatable activity] in [location],
smooth gimbal tracking shot following the action,
golden hour natural lighting with warm tones,
35mm cinematic lens with shallow depth of field,
modern travel vlog aesthetic in the style of Sam Kolder/Peter McKinnon,
warm orange and teal color grade,
inspirational and aspirational mood,
mix of 24fps cinematic and 60fps slow-motion b-roll,
ambient location sound + cinematic music score,
16:9 horizontal, 4K UHD, professional YouTube quality
```

## 3. Instagram Feed Video

- **Square Feed**: 1:1 (1080x1080)

- **Reels**: 9:16 (1080x1920) — xem mục TikTok
- **Duration**: 3-90s feed, 60-90s reels

```
Square 1:1 medium shot of [subject and product],
centered composition with breathing space all sides,

[brand color palette] color grading,
elegant slow camera push-in or subtle motion,
24fps smooth cinematic feel,
balanced for both phone and desktop viewing,
1080x1080 minimum, no text overlay (added via IG tools)
```

## 4. Facebook Video Ads

### Specs
- **Feed**: 4:5 hoặc 1:1 (best mobile coverage)
- **Stories**: 9:16
- **In-stream**: 16:9
- **Duration**: 6-15s ads sweet spot, max 240 minutes
- **FPS**: 30

### Đặc điểm
- **Sound-off design**: 85% xem không tiếng → captions/visual storytelling
- **Hook 3 giây đầu** quan trọng cho Facebook
- **Caption an toàn**: Bottom 20% có thể bị che

### Template Facebook Ad
```
4:5 vertical-first medium shot designed for sound-off viewing,
[clear visual demonstration of product benefit] in first 3 seconds,
text overlay-friendly with clean composition,
bright high-key lighting for thumb-stopping visibility,
vibrant color grading suited to brand,
quick cuts every 2-3 seconds maintaining attention,
30fps smooth motion,
captions and graphics added in post-production,
optimized for Facebook News Feed mobile viewing,
1080x1350 resolution
```

## 8. TVC Truyền hình (Vietnam)

### Specs
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 horizontal
- **Resolution**: HD 1920x1080 (broadcast standard)
- **Duration**: 15s, 30s, 45s, 60s standard slots
- **FPS**: 25 (PAL Vietnam), hoặc 24 (cinematic)
- **Audio**: Stereo mix, broadcast safe

### Đặc điểm Vietnam TVC
- Sản phẩm xuất hiện trong 5s đầu hoặc cuối
- Logo phải hiển thị 2-3s cuối
- Kết hợp tagline rõ ràng
- Chú ý quy định quảng cáo (không so sánh, không gây hiểu nhầm)

### Template Vietnamese TVC
```
Cinematic 16:9 30-second TVC opening with [emotional hook scene],
medium shot of [Vietnamese family/protagonist] in relatable setting,
smooth dolly camera revealing [product] naturally integrated into scene,
warm golden hour lighting suggesting happiness/family,
vibrant Vietnamese-friendly color palette,
emotional and aspirational mood with cultural authenticity,
24fps cinematic motion with strategic slow-motion product moments,
sound design: voiceover in Vietnamese + emotional music + product SFX,
clear product hero shot at 25-second mark,
brand logo and tagline reveal in final 3 seconds,
1920x1080 broadcast quality
```

## 9. Cinema / Theater (21:9 Cinemascope)

### Specs
- **Aspect Ratio**: 2.39:1 (cinemascope)
- **Resolution**: 4K (4096x1716) hoặc 2K
- **Duration**: Varies (short film: 3-30min, feature: 90+min)
- **FPS**: 24fps cinematic (định nghĩa "phim")
- **Audio**: 5.1 surround sound

### Template Cinematic
```
Cinemascope 21:9 wide establishing shot of [epic landscape/scene],
slow crane movement revealing scale and grandeur,
dramatic golden hour or blue hour lighting,
anamorphic lens with horizontal flares and oval bokeh,
desaturated cinematic color grade with selective vibrance,
epic and contemplative mood,
24fps true cinematic motion with subtle film grain,
IMAX-quality production value,
in the style of [Christopher Nolan / Denis Villeneuve / Roger Deakins],
4096x1716 cinema-ready resolution,
suitable for theatrical projection
```

## 10. Music Video

### Specs
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 hoặc 9:16 (modern vertical MVs)
- **Resolution**: 4K
- **Duration**: 2-5 phút (theo độ dài bài hát)
- **FPS**: 24 (cinematic) hoặc 60 (modern smooth)

### Phong cách MV theo thể loại nhạc

| Thể loại | Visual |
|----------|--------|
| **Pop** | Vibrant, dance, multiple sets, bright |
| **Hip-hop** | Urban, gritty, slow-mo, fashion |
| **R&B** | Sensual, warm tones, intimate |
| **Indie** | Aesthetic, lo-fi, dreamy |
| **K-pop** | Highly produced, sets, choreography |
| **V-pop** | Vietnamese cultural elements + modern |
| **Rock** | High energy, performance, fast cuts |
| **EDM** | Festival, lights, particles, bass drops |
| **Acoustic** | Natural, intimate, simple |

### Template Music Video Cinematic
```
[16:9 or 9:16] cinematic music video shot of [artist/scene],
[performance / narrative / abstract] visual storytelling,
[lighting style matching genre],
[camera movement synchronized with beat],
[color grading suited to song mood],
[anamorphic / vintage / modern] aesthetic,
24fps with strategic 60fps slow-motion on key musical moments,
synchronized to music beat with [hard cuts / smooth transitions],
[mood] atmosphere matching song emotion,
in the style of [reference director: Hiro Murai / Director X / Dave Meyers],
4K resolution, professional music video production
```

### V-Pop / K-Pop specific
```
Highly stylized music video for [V-pop/K-pop] song,
multiple sets and outfit changes implied,
choreography with [number] dancers in [setting],
extremely polished color grading with [palette],
mix of wide group shots and intimate close-ups of artist,
synchronized camera movement to choreography,
high-fashion aesthetic with luxurious props,
24fps cinematic with seamless transitions between scenes,
trendy modern visual effects (lens flares, particles),
4K UHD, broadcast and YouTube ready
```
