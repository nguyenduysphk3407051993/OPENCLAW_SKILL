## 1. Cinematic Film

### Phong cách theo đạo diễn

| Director | Signature Style |
|----------|----------------|
| **Christopher Nolan** | IMAX scale, practical effects, cool blue tones, time-bending, orchestral swells |
| **Denis Villeneuve** | Atmospheric vastness, monochrome moments, Roger Deakins lighting |
| **Wes Anderson** | Symmetrical center compositions, pastel palette, deadpan, miniatures |
| **Wong Kar-wai** | Saturated reds/greens, slow-motion, neon, melancholic |
| **Quentin Tarantino** | Trunk shots, vintage 35mm, vibrant blood, music sync |
| **David Fincher** | Cool greens, shadow-heavy, precise camera, dread atmosphere |
| **Stanley Kubrick** | Symmetry, one-point perspective, slow zoom, dolly |
| **Wes Anderson** | Pastel, symmetrical, vintage aesthetic, dollhouse |
| **Greta Gerwig** | Warm naturalistic, character-focused, intimate |
| **Bong Joon-ho** | Genre-mixing, subtle dolly, social commentary visuals |
| **Park Chan-wook** | Stylized violence, beautiful composition, vivid colors |
| **A24 aesthetic** | Indie, atmospheric, psychological, character-driven |

### Template Cinematic Drama
```
Cinematic [shot type] of [protagonist] in [emotional situation],
[camera movement] capturing [emotional moment],
[time of day] with [lighting style] creating [mood],
[anamorphic 35mm/50mm] lens with shallow depth of field,
[color grade: teal-orange / desaturated / warm tones],
contemplative and emotionally resonant atmosphere,
in the style of [reference director] cinematography,
24fps with subtle film grain texture,
IMAX-quality 4K production value,
[ambient sound + score description],
21:9 cinemascope or 16:9 widescreen
```

### Template Pop MV
```
High-concept pop music video shot of [artist] performing in [stylized set],
multiple choreographed sequences with [number] outfit changes implied,
synchronized camera choreography to beat with whip pans on drops,
high-fashion lighting with hard shadows and rim light,
saturated vibrant color palette of [colors],
modern glossy aesthetic in the style of [reference director],
24fps with 60fps slow-motion on hero moments,
seamless transitions between scenes,
ambitious production value with [props/sets/effects],
4K, 16:9 or 9:16 vertical for short-form release
```

### Template Hip-hop MV
```
Cinematic hip-hop music video featuring [artist] in [urban location/luxury setting],
slow dolly tracking shots with anamorphic lens,
dramatic low-angle hero shots establishing power,
golden hour or neon nighttime lighting,
desaturated film noir or vibrant flex aesthetic,
slow-motion on every other beat for impact,
in the style of Hype Williams / Cole Bennett,
moody atmospheric with confident energy,
24fps with strategic 120fps slow-motion,
```

| **Insurance/Financial** | Warm, family, trust, stories | Allstate, Geico |

### Template Luxury TVC
```
Cinematic 30-second luxury commercial,
extreme close-up of [premium product detail] with macro lens,
slow elegant camera movement (dolly or orbit),
moody dramatic lighting with single key light,
anamorphic horizontal flares and oval bokeh,
sophisticated muted color grade with selective vibrance,
intimate aspirational atmosphere,
in the style of Chanel / Louis Vuitton commercials,
24fps with strategic 120fps slow-motion product moments,
minimal sound design: subtle ambient + sophisticated score,
brand reveal in final 3 seconds with logo and tagline,
21:9 cinemascope, 4K
```

## 4. Documentary

### Phong cách

| Style | Đặc điểm |
|-------|----------|
| **Observational (Cinema Verité)** | Fly-on-wall, no narration, raw |
| **Expository** | Narrator-driven, informative |
| **Participatory** | Filmmaker visible in frame |
| **Reflexive** | Self-aware, breaks fourth wall |
| **Performative** | Subjective, personal POV |

### Tham chiếu
- **National Geographic**: Wildlife, landscape, expert narration
- **BBC Earth/Planet Earth**: Epic nature, slow-motion, Attenborough
- **Netflix Docs**: Polished, dramatic, character-driven
- **Werner Herzog**: Existential, strange, philosophical narration
- **Ken Burns**: Archival, slow zoom on photos, period music
- **Errol Morris**: Reenactments, interview-driven

### Template Wildlife Doc
```
National Geographic style wildlife documentary shot,
extreme telephoto lens capturing [animal] in natural habitat,
slow steady camera with subtle handheld feel,
golden hour natural lighting,
deep focus showing animal in environmental context,
warm naturalistic color grade,
patient observational mood respecting wildlife,
24fps with 240fps super slow-motion on critical moments,
ambient natural sound + David Attenborough-style narration described,
sweeping orchestral score on reveals,
16:9, 4K UHD, broadcast documentary quality
```

### Template Human Documentary
```
Observational documentary medium close-up of [subject] in [authentic setting],
handheld camera with subtle natural movement,
available natural light from windows/practical sources,
authentic unposed expression and behavior,
naturalistic color grade preserving real tones,
intimate respectful atmosphere,
24fps real-time pacing letting moments breathe,
ambient location sound (no music in some scenes),
in the style of cinema verité / Frederick Wiseman,
16:9 4K, authentic documentary aesthetic
```

## 7. Tutorial / Educational

```
medium close-up with overhead inserts of demonstration details,

neutral white background or relevant clean setting,
clear and approachable color grade,
patient instructional pace with strategic close-ups,
30fps real-time with no slow-motion (for clarity),
clear narration audio described + minimal background music,
text overlay-friendly composition with clear focus area,

16:9, 4K, professional educational quality
```
