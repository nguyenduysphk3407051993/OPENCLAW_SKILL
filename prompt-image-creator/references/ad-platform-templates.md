## TikTok Ads

### Kích thước & Tỉ lệ
- **Feed Ad**: 9:16 (1080x1920 tối thiểu, 2160x3840 khuyến nghị)
- **Spark Ad**: 9:16
- **Top View**: 9:16
- Safe zone: Tránh 150px trên, 440px dưới (UI elements)

### Phong cách đặc trưng
- **Aesthetic**: Raw, authentic, UGC-style (không quá polished)
- **Energy**: High-energy, fast-paced visual cues
- **Trend**: Theo trend hiện tại (AI-generated, Y2K, cottagecore, clean girl...)
- **Demographics**: Gen-Z primary (18-24), Millennials secondary
- **Color**: Bold, saturated, neon accents, gradient backgrounds
- **Text**: Large, centered, TikTok-native fonts (bold sans-serif)

### Prompt Template cốt lõi
```
[Subject description], TikTok-style vertical composition, 
authentic UGC aesthetic, vibrant and punchy colors, 
ring light reflection, dynamic angle, 
trendy Gen-Z visual language, energetic mood,
text-safe zones at top and bottom of frame,
shot on iPhone aesthetic with professional quality,
4K vertical 2160x3840, 9:16 aspect ratio
```

### Sub-styles
- **TikTok Shop**: Product-centric, before/after, unboxing feel
- **TikTok Brand**: Lifestyle + product integration, aesthetic backgrounds
- **TikTok Creator**: Face + product, selfie angle, expressive

## Facebook Ads

### Kích thước & Tỉ lệ
- **Feed Post**: 1:1 (2048x2048) hoặc 4:5 (1638x2048)
- **Story Ad**: 9:16 (2160x3840)
- **Carousel**: 1:1 per card
- **Cover Photo**: 16:9 (1640x924)
- **Event Cover**: 16:9 (1920x1080)
- Text rule: Giữ text dưới 20% diện tích ảnh

### Phong cách đặc trưng
- **Aesthetic**: Professional yet approachable, trustworthy
- **Demographics**: Broad (25-55), family-oriented, community
- **Color**: Warm, inviting, brand-consistent, avoid Facebook blue clash
- **Composition**: Clear focal point, product/benefit immediately visible
- **CTA**: Visual hierarchy leading to action button area

### Prompt Template cốt lõi
```
[Subject description], professional product/lifestyle photography,
warm inviting tones, Facebook-optimized composition,
clear visual hierarchy with prominent focal point,
clean background with brand-consistent colors,
space for CTA button at bottom,
relatable and trustworthy mood,
high quality 4K, [1:1 or 4:5] aspect ratio
```

### Sub-styles
- **Facebook Marketplace**: Clean product shot, price-visible composition
- **Facebook Lead Gen**: Human faces, trust signals, benefit-focused
- **Facebook Retargeting**: Product close-up, urgency visual cues
- **Facebook Community**: Group activities, diversity, warm moments

## Instagram Ads

### Kích thước & Tỉ lệ
- **Feed Square**: 1:1 (2048x2048)
- **Feed Portrait**: 4:5 (1638x2048)
- **Story/Reels**: 9:16 (2160x3840)
- **Carousel**: 1:1 hoặc 4:5 per slide
- **IGTV Cover**: 1:1.55

### Phong cách đặc trưng
- **Aesthetic**: Curated, aspirational, visually cohesive
- **Demographics**: 18-34, trend-conscious, visual-first
- **Color**: Cohesive feed aesthetic, moody/warm/pastel trends
- **Composition**: Instagram-worthy, share-worthy, save-worthy
- **Quality**: High production value, editorial feel

### Prompt Template cốt lõi
```
[Subject description], Instagram-worthy aesthetic,
[moody/warm/pastel/minimal] color grading,
aspirational lifestyle photography,
curated composition with visual balance,
high production value, editorial quality,
cohesive color palette of [specific colors],
[4:5 portrait or 1:1 square], 4K resolution
```

### Sub-styles
- **Instagram Aesthetic Feed**: Cohesive color story, grid-planned
- **Instagram Reels Cover**: Eye-catching, text overlay friendly
- **Instagram Shopping**: Product tags compatible, clean isolation
- **Instagram Collab**: Split screen or dual subject, partnership feel

## YouTube Ads

### Kích thước & Tỉ lệ
- **Thumbnail**: 16:9 (1920x1080 tối thiểu, 3840x2160 khuyến nghị)
- **Banner/Channel Art**: 16:9 (2560x1440)
- **End Screen**: 16:9 safe zone
- **Community Post**: 1:1 hoặc 16:9

### Phong cách Thumbnail
- **Aesthetic**: High contrast, exaggerated, immediately readable
- **Face**: Large, expressive face (surprise, excitement, curiosity)
- **Text**: Max 3-5 words, huge bold font, outline/shadow
- **Color**: Complementary colors, red+yellow, blue+orange
- **Composition**: 2-3 elements maximum, clean visual hierarchy
- **Background**: Solid color, gradient, or heavily blurred

### Prompt Template cốt lõi
```
[Subject description], YouTube thumbnail style,
extreme high contrast, bold vibrant colors,
exaggerated facial expression showing [emotion],
clean simple composition with maximum 3 visual elements,
large readable visual hierarchy,
complementary color scheme [color1] and [color2],
thick outlines on key elements,
16:9 aspect ratio, 4K resolution 3840x2160
```

## LinkedIn Ads

### Kích thước & Tỉ lệ
- **Single Image**: 1.91:1 (1200x627) hoặc 1:1 (1080x1080)
- **Carousel**: 1:1 (1080x1080) per card
- **Video Thumbnail**: 16:9
- **Company Page Cover**: 4:1 (1128x191)

### Phong cách đặc trưng
- **Aesthetic**: Corporate, professional, authoritative yet human
- **Demographics**: B2B professionals, decision-makers, 25-55
- **Color**: Blue, navy, white, subtle accent colors, clean
- **Composition**: Professional headshots, data visualizations, clean infographics
- **Mood**: Confident, innovative, trustworthy, growth-oriented

### Prompt Template cốt lõi
```
[Subject description], professional corporate photography,
clean modern aesthetic, LinkedIn-appropriate,
blue and neutral color palette with [accent color],
confident and innovative mood,
polished business environment,
data-driven visual elements if applicable,
trust-building composition,
[1.91:1 or 1:1] aspect ratio, high resolution
```

## Pinterest Ads

### Kích thước & Tỉ lệ
- **Standard Pin**: 2:3 (1000x1500)
- **Long Pin**: 1:2.1 (1000x2100)
- **Square Pin**: 1:1 (1000x1000)
- **Idea Pin**: 9:16

### Phong cách đặc trưng
- **Aesthetic**: Aspirational, DIY-friendly, save-worthy, tutorial
- **Demographics**: Women 25-44 primary, home/fashion/food/travel
- **Color**: Warm, inviting, seasonal palettes
- **Composition**: Vertical, text overlay at top, step-by-step friendly
- **Mood**: Inspirational, achievable, beautiful yet approachable

### Prompt Template cốt lõi
```
[Subject description], Pinterest-worthy aesthetic,
warm inviting colors, aspirational yet achievable,
vertical 2:3 composition with text space at top,
[lifestyle/tutorial/inspiration] photography style,
soft natural lighting, organized and beautiful,
save-worthy visual appeal,
2:3 aspect ratio, high resolution
```

## E-commerce Platforms (Shopee/Lazada/Tiki)

### Kích thước
- **Main Product**: 1:1 (800x800 tối thiểu, 2048x2048 khuyến nghị)
- **Banner**: 16:9 hoặc platform-specific
- **Flash Sale**: Varies, bold and urgent

### Phong cách
- **Product Main**: Clean white background, product fills 80%+ frame
- **Lifestyle**: Product in use, aspirational context
- **Sale/Promo**: Bold colors (red, orange), urgency visual cues
- **Review/Testimonial**: Before/after, real results

### Prompt Template
```
[Product description], e-commerce product photography,
pure white background, product centered filling 85% of frame,
professional studio lighting, sharp focus on every detail,
color-accurate representation, no shadows or minimal reflection,
clean and professional, ready for online marketplace,
1:1 square format, 4K resolution 4096x4096
```

## Zalo Ads (Vietnam-specific)

### Kích thước
- **Zalo Article Banner**: 16:9 (1200x628)
- **Zalo Shop**: 1:1 (600x600 tối thiểu)
- **Official Account Cover**: 16:9

### Phong cách
- **Aesthetic**: Friendly, local, Vietnamese cultural elements
- **Demographics**: Vietnamese users 18-45, mobile-first
- **Color**: Zalo blue (#0068FF) compatible, warm local tones
- **Composition**: Mobile-optimized, text readable on small screens
- **Mood**: Trustworthy, local community feel, Vietnamese identity
