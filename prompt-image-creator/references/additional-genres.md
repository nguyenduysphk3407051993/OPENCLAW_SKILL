## 1. Book Cover

### Kích thước & Tỉ lệ
- **Sách giấy (Print)**: 2:3 (1800x2700) hoặc 5:8 (1500x2400)
- **Ebook/Kindle**: 1.6:1 (1600x2560)
- **Audiobook**: 1:1 (3000x3000)
- **Wattpad/online**: 2:3 (512x800 minimum)

### Phong cách theo thể loại sách

| Thể loại | Visual Style | Color | Typography |
|----------|--------------|-------|------------|
| **Văn học/Tiểu thuyết** | Symbolic imagery, atmospheric | Muted, sophisticated | Serif elegant |
| **Trinh thám/Thriller** | Dark, mysterious silhouettes | Black, red, gold accents | Bold sans-serif |
| **Lãng mạn** | Dreamy figures, soft focus | Pastel, warm | Script + serif mix |
| **Khoa học viễn tưởng** | Futuristic, cosmic, tech | Neon blue, purple, black | Geometric sans |
| **Fantasy** | Magical landscapes, characters | Rich jewel tones | Decorative fantasy fonts |
| **Self-help/Business** | Minimalist, conceptual | Bold solid colors | Modern sans-serif |
| **Sách trẻ em** | Vibrant illustrations | Bright primary colors | Playful rounded |
| **Học thuật/Giáo trình** | Clean, professional | Subdued, academic | Classic serif |

### Prompt Template cốt lõi
```
Book cover design for [genre] novel titled "[title]", 
[symbolic central image/character/scene description],
[atmospheric/dramatic/dreamy] mood,
[color palette] with [accent colors],
clear typographic hierarchy with space for title at top and author name at bottom,
spine and back-cover safe zones considered,
[print-ready/digital] format,
2:3 vertical aspect ratio, 300 DPI print quality, 1800x2700 resolution
```

### Ví dụ cho từng dòng sách
- **Trinh thám VN**: "Foggy Hanoi old quarter at midnight, lone figure in trench coat under street lamp, vintage typewriter on table, deep noir atmosphere"
- **Tiểu thuyết tâm lý**: "Single empty chair facing window with rain, soft natural light, melancholic mood, minimalist composition"

## 2. Album Cover

### Kích thước
- **Streaming platforms** (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music): 1:1 (3000x3000 minimum)
- **Vinyl record**: 1:1 (3600x3600 for 12" print)
- **CD jewel case**: 1:1 (1500x1500)
- **Single/EP**: 1:1 (3000x3000)

### Phong cách theo thể loại nhạc

| Thể loại | Visual Direction |
|----------|------------------|
| **Pop** | Bright, glossy, portrait-focused, trendy aesthetic |
| **Hip-hop/Rap** | Bold typography, urban photography, gold/luxury, gritty |
| **Indie/Alternative** | Lo-fi, film grain, dreamy, abstract, vintage feel |
| **EDM/Electronic** | Neon, futuristic, geometric, glitch art, cosmic |
| **Rock/Metal** | Dark, dramatic, high contrast, rebellious imagery |
| **R&B/Soul** | Warm tones, sensual, atmospheric, intimate portraits |
| **Jazz/Classical** | Elegant, minimal, monochrome, vintage typography |
| **K-Pop/V-Pop** | Cute/cool aesthetic, idol-focused, concept-driven, glossy |
| **Acoustic/Folk** | Natural, organic textures, handcrafted feel |
| **Lo-fi/Chillhop** | Anime-inspired, cozy scenes, nostalgic, muted colors |

### Prompt Template
```
Album cover artwork for [genre] music, 
[central concept: portrait/landscape/abstract/symbolic],
[mood: dreamy/aggressive/romantic/cosmic/nostalgic],
[color palette] with [textures/grain/effects],
striking visual that represents the album's theme of [theme],
square 1:1 composition with focal point centered or rule-of-thirds,
typography-friendly with negative space for artist name and album title,
gallery-quality artistic vision, 
3000x3000 resolution, suitable for streaming platforms and vinyl printing
```

## 3. Wallpaper

### Kích thước theo thiết bị
- **Desktop 4K**: 16:9 (3840x2160)
- **Desktop 5K**: 16:9 (5120x2880)
- **Desktop ultrawide**: 21:9 (5120x2160)
- **iPhone 15 Pro Max**: 9:19.5 (1290x2796)
- **iPhone Standard**: 9:16 (1170x2532)
- **Android**: 9:16 (1080x1920 to 1440x3200)
- **iPad Pro**: 4:3 (2732x2048)
- **Apple Watch**: 1:1.2 (396x484)
- **Lock screen**: 9:16 with focal point in upper third (avoid clock area)

### Phong cách wallpaper phổ biến

| Style | Mô tả |
|-------|-------|
| **Minimalist** | Single subject, lots of negative space, monochrome |
| **Abstract** | Geometric shapes, gradients, fluid art, no recognizable subject |
| **Nature** | Landscapes, mountains, oceans, forests, cosmic |
| **Cyberpunk** | Neon cityscapes, future tech, holographic |
| **Dark Mode** | Black/very dark backgrounds, subtle accents (OLED-friendly) |
| **Aesthetic/Y2K** | Retro futurism, vaporwave, pastel grids |
| **Anime** | Character art, scenic anime backgrounds, Studio Ghibli style |
| **Gradient** | Smooth color gradients, mesh gradients, vibrant blends |
| **Liquid/Fluid** | Marble textures, paint mixing, oil swirls |
| **Iconic** | Famous artworks, movies, characters reimagined |

### Prompt Template
```
[Style] wallpaper design for [device type: desktop/phone/tablet],
[subject/concept description],
[atmospheric details, lighting, color palette],
optimized for [aspect ratio] with focal point [position to avoid icons/clock],
[depth/dimension/flat] composition,
sharp at 100% zoom, no text or watermarks,
[OLED-friendly deep blacks if dark mode],
[resolution: 4K/5K/8K] [aspect ratio],
seamless edges suitable for tiling if applicable
```

### Pro tips
- **iPhone wallpaper**: Để chủ thể chính ở giữa hoặc dưới (clock/widget ở trên)
- **Desktop**: Chừa khu vực dock và icon trống
- **Lock screen**: Phần trên 1/3 nên đơn giản (đồng hồ hiển thị)

## 4. Game / Concept Art

### Kích thước
- **Key Art (poster game)**: 2:3 (3000x4500)
- **Splash Screen**: 16:9 (3840x2160)
- **Loading Screen**: 16:9
- **Character Sheet**: A3 (3508x4961)
- **Environment Concept**: 16:9 hoặc 21:9
- **Steam Capsule**: 92:43 (920x430)

### Phong cách concept art

| Style | Mô tả | Ví dụ tham chiếu |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| **Realistic AAA** | Photorealistic, hyper-detailed | God of War, Last of Us |
| **Stylized 3D** | Cartoonish nhưng tinh xảo | Fortnite, Overwatch |
| **Painterly** | Brush strokes, oil painting feel | League of Legends splash art |
| **Pixel Art** | 8-bit/16-bit, retro game | Stardew Valley, Hollow Knight |
| **Cel-shaded** | Comic book style 3D | Borderlands, Genshin Impact |
| **Dark Fantasy** | Gothic, grimdark | Dark Souls, Bloodborne |
| **JRPG Anime** | Vibrant anime aesthetic | Final Fantasy, Persona |
| **Sci-fi** | Futuristic, tech-heavy | Cyberpunk 2077, Mass Effect |

### Prompt Template — Character Concept
```
Game character concept art, [character name/role: warrior/mage/etc.],
[detailed appearance: face, hair, body type, age],
[outfit description with materials, weapons, accessories],
[pose: dynamic action/heroic stance/idle T-pose for reference],
[style: AAA realistic/stylized 3D/painterly/anime],
[atmospheric background or neutral concept art background],
professional video game industry quality,
multi-angle reference sheet style if needed,
ArtStation-worthy concept art,
4K resolution, suitable for production pipeline
```

### Prompt Template — Environment
```
Game environment concept art, [location type: dungeon/city/forest/space station],
[atmospheric details: time of day, weather, mood],
[architectural/natural elements with rich detail],
[lighting: dramatic/atmospheric/cinematic],
[color palette suiting the game's mood],
epic scale with sense of depth and exploration,
matte painting quality,
[style: realistic/stylized/painterly],
16:9 widescreen cinematic composition,
8K resolution, ArtStation portfolio quality
```

## 5. Children's Illustration

### Kích thước
- **Sách giấy bé (Picture book)**: A4 (2480x3508) 300DPI
- **Sách hardcover**: 8.5x11 inch ratio
- **Truyện tranh thiếu nhi**: A4 portrait
- **Educational poster**: A3 (3508x4961)
- **Flashcard**: 1:1.4

### Phong cách theo độ tuổi

| Độ tuổi | Style | Đặc điểm |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **0-3 tuổi** | Simple shapes, high contrast | Big shapes, primary colors, friendly faces |
| **3-6 tuổi** | Cute, rounded, vibrant | Watercolor, expressive characters, magical |
| **6-9 tuổi** | More detail, storytelling | Adventure scenes, character variety, action |
| **9-12 tuổi** | Sophisticated illustration | Manga-influenced, complex scenes, emotional depth |

### Phong cách tham chiếu
- **Studio Ghibli**: Whimsical, magical, hand-drawn quality
- **Disney Classic**: Charming, expressive, family-friendly
- **Watercolor Storybook**: Beatrix Potter, Eric Carle style
- **Modern Flat**: Oliver Jeffers, contemporary minimalist
- **Vietnamese Folk**: Tranh dân gian Đông Hồ, traditional motifs
- **Pixar Style**: 3D rendered, cinematic, big eyes

### Prompt Template
```
Children's book illustration for ages [age range],
[scene description: characters, setting, action],
adorable [main character] with expressive features,
[Style: watercolor/digital painting/Studio Ghibli inspired/flat illustration],
warm friendly color palette of [colors],
educational/storytelling composition,
soft natural lighting, gentle and comforting mood,
detailed background showing [environment],
suitable for picture book printing,
A4 portrait or landscape, 300 DPI, child-safe imagery
```

### Lưu ý quan trọng
- **Tránh**: Bạo lực, hình ảnh đáng sợ, đôi mắt đáng sợ
- **Đảm bảo**: Tỷ lệ thân hình đáng yêu, tay chân tròn trịa, mắt to biểu cảm
- **Văn hóa Việt**: Có thể thêm áo bà ba, áo dài, nón lá, làng quê VN, tre, sen

## 6. 3D Render / CGI

### Kích thước
- **Product 3D**: 1:1 (4096x4096)
- **Architectural Visualization**: 16:9 (4K-8K)
- **Cinematic Render**: 21:9 (5120x2160)
- **Print-ready**: 300 DPI, varies

### Phong cách 3D phổ biến

| Style | Engine/Software | Đặc điểm |
|-------|-----------------|----------|
| **Octane Render** | Photorealistic | Premium product, jewelry, cars |
| **Cinema 4D + Redshift** | Stylized realistic | Motion graphics, branding |
| **Blender Cycles** | Open source realistic | Indie projects, character |
| **Unreal Engine 5** | Cinematic real-time | Game cinematics, archviz |
| **V-Ray** | Architectural standard | Interior, exterior buildings |
| **ZBrush sculpt** | Organic detail | Characters, creatures |
| **Soft 3D / Clay** | Cute, friendly | UI illustrations, branding |
| **Low Poly** | Geometric simple | Indie game, NFT, modern |
| **Isometric 3D** | Tech illustration | Apps, infographics, dashboards |

### Prompt Template — Product 3D
```
3D rendered product visualization of [product name],
hyper-realistic materials with [glossy/matte/metallic/glass] finish,
[Octane/Cinema 4D/Blender] render quality,
studio HDRI lighting with subtle reflections,
[color] background with smooth gradient,
sharp ray-traced shadows and reflections,
sub-surface scattering for translucent materials,
extreme detail on textures and surfaces,
commercial advertising quality,
1:1 square or 16:9, 4K resolution, photorealistic CGI
```

### Prompt Template — Stylized 3D
```
Soft 3D rendered illustration, [subject description],
clay-like rounded geometry, friendly playful aesthetic,
pastel color palette of [colors],
Cinema 4D render style with Redshift,
soft global illumination, ambient occlusion,
chunky proportions, exaggerated cute features,
modern 3D illustration trend,
isometric or 3/4 perspective,
clean minimal background,
suitable for tech branding/UI illustration,
4K resolution, vibrant and approachable
```

## 7. Anime / Manga Style

### Kích thước
- **Manga page**: B5 (2480x3543) or A5
- **Anime key visual**: 16:9 (3840x2160)
- **Light novel cover**: 2:3 (1500x2250)
- **Character splash**: 9:16 portrait
- **Webtoon (vertical)**: 800x1280 per panel, infinite scroll

### Phong cách theo studio/thể loại

| Style | Đặc điểm | Ví dụ |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Studio Ghibli** | Soft watercolor, nature-focused, dreamy | Spirited Away, Totoro |
| **Makoto Shinkai** | Hyper-detailed backgrounds, dramatic light | Your Name, Weathering With You |
| **Kyoto Animation** | Clean lines, expressive faces, vibrant | Violet Evergarden, K-On |
| **Shonen Action** | Dynamic poses, speed lines, intense | Demon Slayer, JJK, AOT |
| **Shojo Romance** | Soft, sparkly, flower motifs, slim figures | Fruits Basket, Kimi ni Todoke |
| **Seinen Mature** | Realistic proportions, dark themes | Berserk, Vagabond |
| **Chibi/Super Deformed** | Cute compressed proportions | Stickers, mascots |
| **90s Retro Anime** | Cel animation feel, grainy, nostalgic | Sailor Moon, Evangelion |
| **Modern Webtoon** | Vertical, full color, smooth gradients | Tower of God, Solo Leveling |

### Prompt Template — Anime Character
```
Anime style illustration in [Studio Ghibli/Makoto Shinkai/shonen/shojo] aesthetic,
[character description: gender, age, hair color/style, eye color, outfit],
[expression and pose],
[setting: school rooftop/cherry blossom park/cyberpunk city/fantasy realm],
[lighting: golden hour/blue hour/dramatic],
detailed anime-style face with expressive eyes,
[color palette suited to mood],
clean vector-like lineart with cel shading,
dynamic composition,
high-quality anime key visual,
[2:3 portrait or 16:9 landscape], 4K resolution
```

### Prompt Template — Manga Page
```
Manga page in [shonen/shojo/seinen] style,
black and white ink illustration with screentones,
[number] panel layout: [describe each panel briefly],
[character name] showing [emotion/action] with dynamic motion lines,
[Japanese-inspired or Vietnamese setting],
detailed crosshatching and ink shadows,
sharp lineart, expressive character faces,
proper manga panel hierarchy and reading flow [right-to-left for traditional manga],
B5 page format, 2480x3543, print-ready 300 DPI
```

### Prompt Template — Webtoon Panel
```
Webtoon-style vertical illustration, [scene description],
full color digital painting,
[character pose and expression],
modern smooth digital art technique,
soft cel-shading with gradient lighting,
[color palette],
clean readable composition for mobile vertical scrolling,
dramatic atmosphere with cinematic lighting,
9:16 vertical or longer scrolling format,
800x1280 per panel, optimized for phone reading
```

### Tham chiếu chuyên nghiệp
- **For shonen**: "in the style of Demon Slayer ufotable animation"
- **For Ghibli**: "Studio Ghibli watercolor background painting style"
- **For Shinkai**: "Makoto Shinkai detailed background, sky and clouds"
- **For modern**: "modern anime 2024, high production value, sakuga frame"
