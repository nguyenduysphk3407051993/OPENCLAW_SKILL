## Product Photography

### Flatlay Style
- **Camera**: Top-down (90-degree overhead)
- **Lighting**: Soft diffused natural light or 2-light softbox setup
- **Background**: Clean marble, wood texture, solid color paper
- **Composition**: Grid layout, diagonal arrangement, or organized chaos
- **Props**: Complementary items, fabric swatches, botanical elements
- **Key phrases**: "flatlay product photography, top-down view, organized layout, lifestyle props, soft natural lighting, clean aesthetic"

### Hero/Packshot Style
- **Camera**: Slightly below eye level, 85mm-100mm macro lens
- **Lighting**: 3-point lighting (key, fill, rim) on white sweep
- **Background**: Pure white (#FFFFFF) or gradient gray
- **Composition**: Product centered, generous white space
- **Post-processing**: Clean, sharp, color-accurate, shadow removal
- **Key phrases**: "commercial product photography, clean white background, studio lighting, sharp focus, e-commerce ready, professional packshot"

### Lifestyle Style
- **Camera**: 35-50mm lens, environmental angle
- **Lighting**: Natural window light, golden hour
- **Background**: Real-life setting (kitchen, desk, outdoor)
- **Composition**: Product in use, human interaction, story-telling
- **Key phrases**: "lifestyle product photography, in-context, natural setting, warm tones, authentic feel, product in use"

### 360 / Creative
- **Camera**: Multiple angles, creative perspectives
- **Lighting**: Dramatic single light, colored gels, neon
- **Background**: Abstract, gradient, or floating objects
- **Composition**: Dynamic angles, levitation, splash effects
- **Key phrases**: "creative product photography, levitation effect, dynamic splash, dramatic lighting, neon accents, bold composition"

## Food Photography

### Overhead/Flat Lay
- **Camera**: 90-degree top-down, 35-50mm lens
- **Lighting**: Side natural light from window, diffusion panel
- **Background**: Rustic wood, marble, concrete, linen
- **Composition**: Full spread, multiple dishes, utensils, ingredients scattered
- **Key phrases**: "overhead food photography, flat lay dining table, rustic styling, natural window light, food spread"

### 45-Degree Angle (Classic)
- **Camera**: 45-degree angle, 85-100mm lens, f/2.8-4
- **Lighting**: Side light with bounce card, warm tone
- **Background**: Shallow depth of field, restaurant ambiance
- **Composition**: Hero dish front-center, complementary items soft focus
- **Key phrases**: "appetizing food photography, 45-degree angle, shallow depth of field, warm natural tones, restaurant styling"

### Dark & Moody (Chiaroscuro)
- **Camera**: Low angle or 45-degree, fast prime lens
- **Lighting**: Single directional light, deep shadows, Rembrandt style
- **Background**: Dark wood, slate, black surface
- **Color palette**: Deep browns, burgundy, gold highlights, black
- **Key phrases**: "dark moody food photography, chiaroscuro lighting, dramatic shadows, rich tones, vintage feel, low-key lighting"

### Bright & Airy
- **Camera**: Overhead or 45-degree, slight overexposure
- **Lighting**: Abundant natural light, white reflectors
- **Background**: White marble, light wood, pastel surfaces
- **Color palette**: Whites, pastels, fresh greens, soft pinks
- **Key phrases**: "bright airy food photography, high-key lighting, fresh clean aesthetic, pastel tones, minimalist plating"

### Action/Process Shot
- **Camera**: Fast shutter 1/1000+, continuous shooting
- **Lighting**: Strobe/flash for freeze motion
- **Composition**: Pour, drizzle, sprinkle, steam, crumble mid-action
- **Key phrases**: "food action shot, pouring sauce, chocolate drizzle, flour dust, steam rising, frozen motion, dynamic food photography"

## Portrait Photography

### Studio Portrait
- **Camera**: 85mm f/1.4 or 70-200mm f/2.8
- **Lighting**: Rembrandt, butterfly, split, or loop lighting
- **Background**: Seamless paper (gray, white, black), painted canvas
- **Composition**: Head and shoulders, tight crop, catchlight in eyes
- **Key phrases**: "professional studio portrait, Rembrandt lighting, shallow depth of field, sharp eye focus, clean background"

### Environmental Portrait
- **Camera**: 35-50mm lens, wider framing
- **Lighting**: Available light, supplemented with reflector
- **Background**: Subject's natural environment (office, workshop, home)
- **Composition**: Subject in context, environmental storytelling
- **Key phrases**: "environmental portrait, natural setting, storytelling, authentic, available light, candid moment"

### Headshot (Corporate/Actor)
- **Camera**: 85-135mm, f/4-5.6 for more in focus
- **Lighting**: Clamshell lighting (beauty dish + fill from below)
- **Background**: Solid neutral, slight texture, or out-of-focus office
- **Composition**: Head to upper chest, centered, direct eye contact
- **Key phrases**: "professional headshot, corporate portrait, clean lighting, confident expression, neutral background"

### Editorial/Artistic Portrait
- **Camera**: Various focal lengths for creative effect
- **Lighting**: Creative, mixed light, gels, practical lights
- **Background**: Textured walls, urban decay, architectural elements
- **Composition**: Rule-breaking, negative space, unconventional crops
- **Key phrases**: "editorial portrait, artistic, creative lighting, bold composition, moody atmosphere, fashion-inspired"

### Cinematic Portrait
- **Camera**: Anamorphic lens look, 2.39:1 aspect ratio
- **Lighting**: Motivated lighting (practical sources visible), warm/cool contrast
- **Background**: Bokeh city lights, neon, atmospheric haze
- **Key phrases**: "cinematic portrait, anamorphic bokeh, film grain, warm color grading, atmospheric haze, movie still aesthetic"

## Landscape Photography

### Golden Hour
- **Camera**: 16-35mm wide angle, f/8-11, tripod
- **Lighting**: Warm directional sunlight, long shadows
- **Color palette**: Golden yellows, warm oranges, soft pinks
- **Composition**: Leading lines, foreground interest, rule of thirds
- **Key phrases**: "golden hour landscape, warm sunlight, dramatic sky, long shadows, vibrant colors, wide angle, foreground interest"

### Blue Hour
- **Camera**: Wide angle, f/8, long exposure 10-30 seconds
- **Lighting**: Cool ambient light, city lights starting
- **Color palette**: Deep blues, purples, orange city glow
- **Key phrases**: "blue hour landscape, twilight, cool tones, long exposure, city lights, serene atmosphere, deep blue sky"

### Long Exposure
- **Camera**: ND filter, 30sec-5min exposure, tripod essential
- **Lighting**: Any — effect smooths water and clouds
- **Composition**: Moving elements (water, clouds) vs static (rocks, buildings)
- **Key phrases**: "long exposure landscape, silky smooth water, streaking clouds, minimalist, ethereal, neutral density filter"

### Aerial/Drone
- **Camera**: DJI or equivalent, top-down or 45-degree
- **Composition**: Patterns, textures, scale contrast, abstract from above
- **Key phrases**: "aerial photography, drone shot, bird's eye view, abstract patterns, dramatic scale, top-down perspective"

### Storm/Dramatic Weather
- **Camera**: Fast shutter for lightning, wide angle
- **Lighting**: Dramatic cloud formations, storm light
- **Color palette**: Dark grays, electric blues, ominous greens
- **Key phrases**: "dramatic storm landscape, dark clouds, lightning, moody atmosphere, powerful nature, high contrast"

## Fashion Photography

### Runway/Catwalk
- **Camera**: 70-200mm f/2.8, fast AF, high ISO capability
- **Lighting**: Even front lighting, bright, clean
- **Composition**: Full length, mid-stride, direct front
- **Key phrases**: "runway fashion photography, full length, designer clothing, sharp focus, clean lighting, catwalk pose"

### Editorial/High Fashion
- **Camera**: Medium format or 85mm prime
- **Lighting**: Dramatic, conceptual, mixed techniques
- **Background**: Exotic locations, architectural, surreal sets
- **Composition**: Creative posing, story-driven, artistic
- **Key phrases**: "high fashion editorial, Vogue-inspired, dramatic lighting, conceptual styling, avant-garde, artistic composition"

### Lookbook/E-commerce
- **Camera**: 85mm, f/5.6-8, consistent lighting
- **Lighting**: Soft even lighting, minimal shadows
- **Background**: White or light gray seamless
- **Composition**: Standard poses (front, side, detail shots), full outfit visible
- **Key phrases**: "lookbook photography, clean lighting, e-commerce, full outfit, consistent style, neutral background"

### Street Style
- **Camera**: 35-50mm, fast shooting, candid
- **Lighting**: Natural urban light, street lamps, neon signs
- **Background**: Urban environment, city streets, graffiti walls
- **Composition**: Full length or 3/4, walking or posing casually
- **Key phrases**: "street style fashion, urban setting, candid pose, natural light, authentic, city backdrop, contemporary"

## Architecture Photography

### Interior Design
- **Camera**: Tilt-shift lens or 16-35mm with perspective correction
- **Lighting**: Multiple exposures blended, window light + artificial
- **Composition**: Straight verticals, symmetry, one-point perspective
- **Key phrases**: "interior design photography, vertical lines corrected, natural light, warm ambiance, spacious feel, luxury interior"

### Exterior/Building
- **Camera**: 24mm tilt-shift, blue hour or golden hour
- **Lighting**: Time of day critical, twilight preferred
- **Composition**: Symmetry, leading lines, surrounding context
- **Key phrases**: "architectural photography, modern building, clean lines, blue hour, dramatic sky, symmetrical composition"

### Real Estate
- **Camera**: Wide angle 16-24mm, HDR technique
- **Lighting**: Flash bounced + ambient blend
- **Composition**: Corner shots showing 2 walls, windows visible
- **Key phrases**: "real estate photography, wide angle, bright and airy, HDR, spacious rooms, natural and artificial light blend"

### Minimalist Architecture
- **Camera**: Any focal length, geometric framing
- **Lighting**: Hard directional sunlight creating geometric shadows
- **Composition**: Negative space, geometric shapes, abstract forms
- **Key phrases**: "minimalist architecture, geometric shapes, strong shadows, negative space, abstract, clean lines, modernist"

## Event & Documentary

### Event Coverage
- **Camera**: 24-70mm f/2.8 + 70-200mm f/2.8 dual body
- **Lighting**: Bounce flash, high ISO capability
- **Composition**: Candid moments, reactions, wide establishing shots + details
- **Key phrases**: "event photography, candid moments, energetic atmosphere, ambient lighting, storytelling"

### Wedding
- **Camera**: 35mm f/1.4 + 85mm f/1.2
- **Lighting**: Natural light preferred, off-camera flash for reception
- **Composition**: Emotional moments, details, cinematic wide shots
- **Key phrases**: "wedding photography, romantic, soft bokeh, emotional moment, film-inspired, dreamy, golden hour"

### Documentary/Photojournalism
- **Camera**: 35mm or 50mm prime, unobtrusive
- **Lighting**: Available light only
- **Composition**: Decisive moment, environmental context, raw emotion
- **Key phrases**: "documentary photography, raw authentic, decisive moment, available light, storytelling, unposed, real"

## Macro & Close-up

### Nature Macro
- **Camera**: 100mm macro lens, f/8-16, focus stacking
- **Lighting**: Ring light or twin flash
- **Composition**: Fill frame, isolate subject, abstract textures
- **Key phrases**: "macro photography, extreme close-up, water droplets, insect detail, focus stacking, vivid colors, shallow DOF"

### Product Macro/Detail
- **Camera**: 100mm macro, f/5.6-8
- **Lighting**: Soft directional light, light tent
- **Composition**: Texture detail, material quality, craftsmanship
- **Key phrases**: "macro product detail, texture close-up, material quality, sharp focus, craftsmanship, luxury detail"

## Advertising Photography

### Print Ad
- **Camera**: Medium format or high-res full frame
- **Lighting**: Complex multi-light setup, precise control
- **Composition**: Space for copy, clear visual hierarchy
- **Key phrases**: "commercial advertising photography, product hero, clean composition, text space, professional lighting, brand aligned"

### Digital/Social Ad
- **Camera**: Full frame, various focal lengths
- **Lighting**: Brand-consistent, platform-optimized
- **Composition**: Thumb-stopping, platform-specific safe zones
- **Key phrases**: "digital advertising, social media optimized, eye-catching, scroll-stopping, brand consistent, CTA friendly"

### Billboard/OOH
- **Camera**: Highest resolution available
- **Lighting**: Bold, high contrast, readable from distance
- **Composition**: Ultra simple, one clear message, large elements
- **Key phrases**: "billboard photography, bold visual, high impact, simple composition, readable at distance, outdoor advertising"

## Editorial & Magazine

### Cover Shot
- **Camera**: Medium format, 85-100mm equivalent
- **Lighting**: Beauty lighting (butterfly + fill), hair light
- **Composition**: Space for masthead, coverlines, barcode area
- **Key phrases**: "magazine cover, beauty lighting, space for text, direct gaze, polished, high-end retouching, captivating"

### Feature Spread
- **Camera**: Various — match editorial concept
- **Lighting**: Story-driven, location-appropriate
- **Composition**: Full bleed, gutter awareness, varied layouts
- **Key phrases**: "editorial spread, magazine layout, storytelling, varied compositions, cohesive series, high production value"

## Street Photography

### Classic Street
- **Camera**: 35mm or 28mm, zone focusing, hip shooting
- **Lighting**: Available light, shadows and light play
- **Composition**: Layered scenes, geometric urban elements, human moments
- **Key phrases**: "street photography, candid, urban life, decisive moment, layers, shadows and light, authentic"

### Urban Night
- **Camera**: 35mm f/1.4, high ISO 3200-12800
- **Lighting**: Neon signs, street lamps, reflections on wet pavement
- **Color palette**: Neon pink, electric blue, warm tungsten, deep blacks
- **Key phrases**: "urban night photography, neon lights, wet streets, cyberpunk atmosphere, high ISO, moody, cinematic"

## Fine Art Photography

### Conceptual
- **Camera**: Any — concept drives technique
- **Lighting**: Whatever serves the concept
- **Composition**: Surreal, symbolic, thought-provoking
- **Key phrases**: "fine art photography, conceptual, surreal, symbolic, gallery-quality, thought-provoking, artistic vision"

### Abstract
- **Camera**: Macro or ICM (intentional camera movement)
- **Lighting**: Experimental, light painting, long exposure
- **Composition**: Color, form, texture over representation
- **Key phrases**: "abstract photography, intentional camera movement, light painting, color study, texture, non-representational"

### Double Exposure / Composite
- **Camera**: In-camera or digital composite
- **Composition**: Silhouette + landscape, portrait + texture overlay
- **Key phrases**: "double exposure, composite photography, surreal blend, silhouette overlay, dreamy, artistic, layered imagery"
